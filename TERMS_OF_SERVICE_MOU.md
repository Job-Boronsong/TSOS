## PREAMBLE

This Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU" or "Agreement") sets out the terms and conditions under which Torrential Technologies ("Provider," "We," or "Us") provides the **Torrential School Operations Suite (TSOS)** — a browser-based Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) school management platform — to subscribing educational institutions ("School," "Subscriber," or "You").

Both parties enter this Agreement in good faith, with a shared commitment to improving school administration, data management, and educational outcomes across Ghana and the broader African continent.

By registering, accessing, or using the TSOS platform, the School confirms that it has read, understood, and agrees to be bound by the terms of this MOU.

## PART TWO — SCOPE OF SERVICES

### 2.1 Platform Services Provided
Subject to subscription and payment of applicable fees, the Provider grants the School a non-exclusive, non-transferable licence to access and use the TSOS platform, which includes:

| Module | Description |
|---|---|
| **Student Management** | Enrolment, profiles, photo upload, fee waivers, class history, ID card generation |
| **Class & Teacher Management** | Class assignment, homeroom and subject-teacher mapping, credential management |
| **Attendance** | Student and teacher daily attendance, GPS-based teacher self check-in/check-out |
| **Finance & Fees** | Fee collection records, bus fees, expenditure tracking, payment via Paystack |
| **Timetable** | Weekly class and teacher timetable management |
| **Academic Reports** | Score entry (classwork, tests, homework, project, exams), report cards (Ghana A1–F9 scale) |
| **Insights & Analytics** | Per-student and per-teacher performance dashboards |
| **Feeding/Canteen** | Daily feeding register and feeding fund ledger |
| **Staff Payroll** | Salary profiles, Ghana PAYE computation, SSNIT deductions, payroll run management |
| **Discipline Log** | Incident recording, warnings, detentions, suspensions, commendations |
| **End-of-Year Promotion** | AI-assisted promotion wizard, bulk student promotion/graduation |
| **Announcements & Calendar** | School notices, operational calendar events with staff targeting |
| **Offline-First Operation** | All core functions available without internet; auto-syncs when connectivity returns |
| **Super Admin Portal** | Multi-school oversight for platform administrators |

### 2.2 Services Not Included
The following are outside the scope of this Agreement unless separately arranged:
- Custom software development or bespoke module requests
- Hardware procurement or IT infrastructure support
- Third-party integrations not listed in the platform
- On-site training (remote/online training is available on request)
- Data recovery resulting from actions by the School or its users

## PART THREE — SUBSCRIPTION AND PAYMENT

### 3.1 Subscription Model
Access to the TSOS platform is provided on a prepaid subscription basis. The School selects the number of months at onboarding and renews as needed.

### 3.2 Pricing
| Duration | Discount |
|---|---|
| 1–2 months | Standard rate |
| 3–6 months | 5% discount |
| 7+ months | 10% discount |

The monthly subscription fee is set by the platform administrator and displayed at the point of purchase. Prices are denominated in **Ghana Cedis (GHS)** unless otherwise stated.

### 3.3 Payment Method
Payments are processed securely through **Paystack**. By initiating a payment, the School agrees to Paystack's terms and conditions. The Provider does not store card details.

### 3.4 Subscription Validity and Grace Period
- Subscription starts from the activation date and runs for the paid duration.
- A **3-day grace period** is granted after expiry, during which the School may renew without data loss.
- After the grace period, access is suspended. Data is retained for **90 days** from suspension before permanent deletion.

### 3.5 Renewal Date
**Subscription renewal runs from the payment date.** Upon renewal, the new subscription period starts from the date of payment and extends for the number of months paid, regardless of when the previous subscription expired. Schools are encouraged to renew promptly to avoid service interruption.

### 3.6 Refund Policy
Subscription fees are non-refundable once access has been granted, except where:
- A technical failure by the Provider rendered the platform completely inaccessible for more than 7 consecutive days within the paid period; or
- A duplicate payment was made in error, verified by transaction records.

Refund requests must be submitted in writing within 30 days of the billing date.

### 3.7 Price Changes
The Provider reserves the right to revise subscription pricing. Schools will be notified at least **30 days in advance** via in-platform notice or email. Ongoing subscriptions are unaffected until their next renewal.

## PART FOUR — DATA OWNERSHIP AND PRIVACY

### 4.1 Ownership of School Data
All data entered into the platform by the School — including student records, staff information, financial data, and attendance records — remains the exclusive property of the School. The Provider claims no ownership over School Data.

### 4.2 Provider's Use of Data
The Provider will:
- Use School Data solely to provide and improve the TSOS platform.
- Not sell, rent, or share School Data with third parties for commercial purposes.
- Access School Data only for support, maintenance, or security purposes, and only with the School's consent except where legally required.

### 4.3 Student and Staff Data — Special Obligations
Given that the platform handles personal data of minors (students), the School agrees to:
- Obtain all necessary consents from parents/guardians as required by applicable law.
- Ensure staff using the platform are authorised and aware of data handling obligations.
- Notify the Provider promptly of any data breach or suspected unauthorised access originating from the School's side.

### 4.4 Data Security
The Provider implements industry-standard security measures including:
- Password hashing (bcrypt)
- Session-based authentication with idle timeout
- Account lockout after failed login attempts
- HTTPS-encrypted data transmission
- Regular database backups

Notwithstanding the above, no system is completely immune to breaches. The Provider is not liable for damages arising from security incidents beyond its reasonable control.

### 4.5 Data Retention and Export
Upon subscription cancellation or expiry (after the 90-day retention window), the Provider may permanently delete School Data. Schools are encouraged to export their data before cancellation. The Provider will provide reasonable assistance for data export upon written request.

### 4.6 Compliance with Ghanaian Law
Both parties acknowledge the requirements of Ghana's **Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843)** and agree to comply with its provisions in the collection, processing, storage, and use of personal data.
